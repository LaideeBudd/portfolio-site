ABOUT

I’m a graphic designer based in Nashville, Tennessee, focused on building cohesive brand identities and digital experiences. I approach design as a system, where every element is considered in relation to the whole.

I’m drawn to work where structure and clarity matter, and where small details shape the way something is experienced across different applications. My background across design and development shapes how I approach projects, with a focus on how things are built as much as how they look.

Outside of client work, I’m currently co-founding and designing The Foundation, a narrative-driven board game where story informs both gameplay and visual design.
